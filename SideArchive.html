Privacy Policy for SideArchive

Effective date: September 16, 2026

SideArchive is designed to keep your clipboard history private and stored locally on your Mac.

Information SideArchive Handles

SideArchive reads text that you copy to your Mac’s clipboard so it can display your clipboard history and allow you to organize saved snippets. Clipboard history, pinned items, folders, and settings are stored locally on your device.

Data Collection

SideArchive does not collect, transmit, upload, sell, or share your personal information or clipboard contents. SideArchive does not use analytics, advertising services, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection services.

Data Storage and Deletion

Your information remains on your Mac and is under your control. You can remove clipboard-history entries, pinned items, and saved snippets from within the app. Uninstalling SideArchive removes the app; associated local data may also be removed according to macOS behavior.
